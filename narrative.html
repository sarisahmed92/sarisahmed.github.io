Big Fish (2026)
Dir. Kevin Khamneipur

Role: Editor

In a world where shadows whisper secrets and danger lurks in every corner, a beautiful young singer embarks on a haunting quest for justice. Her parents' murderer, a ruthless gangster, hides within the seedy depths of the city's criminal underbelly. To catch this elusive "big fish," she must plunge headlong into the murky waters of betrayal and corruption.
